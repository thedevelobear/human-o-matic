# 0110. The site looks vaporwave, from original artwork, shaped by Apple's design principles

- **Status:** Accepted. Its tabs are amended by [ADR 0124](0124-the-page-asks-each-round-on-one-card-keeps-its-answers-as-a-draft-and-applies-it-when-the-user-moves-on.md)
- **Date:** 2026-11-14

- The site's look is vaporwave. It replaces the space-age look the site plan's theme section described, which no ADR recorded.
- The look is a style only, drawn in the site's own CSS and SVG: a dusk sky in a gradient, a striped sun, a still wireframe grid floor, each step in a window like an old desktop's with a title bar, and pixel accents such as stepped edges, a progress bar of blocks and a pixel pointer. No image, template, figure, artwork or asset is taken or copied from a reference or anyone else. A title bar carries no button that does nothing. The scene behind the windows is decorative, hidden from assistive technology, and holds no text but the wordmark.
- Fonts are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, self-hosted, each with its `OFL.txt`, and have no Reserved Font Name, so a WOFF2 subset of one keeps its name: Archivo Black for the wordmark and headings, and Space Mono for the windows' title bars, the questions and their answers. Other text is in the platform's font, `system-ui`. Until the owner supplies a logo, the wordmark is the name set in Archivo Black, and the favicon an original SVG of the striped sun.
- Interaction, motion and type follow the principles of the apple-design skill, a skill kept in the owner's Claude Code folder, not in this repository, through the web platform's own tools:
  - **Response:** a control answers on pointer-down, and nothing on the input path waits on a timer, a debounce or an animation. A slider's value and its words follow it while it is dragged.
  - **Motion** is springs, critically damped, damping 1.0 and a response of about 0.35 s, with no bounce, since nothing on the page is flicked or thrown. Each starts from the value on screen and keeps its velocity when its target changes, so it can be interrupted and reversed, and input is never locked while it runs. Only `transform` and `opacity` animate, from a small spring of the page's own on `requestAnimationFrame`, since the site adds no package.
  - **Restraint:** only the current tab's highlight, the answer pointer, a window opening from the control that opened it and closing back to it, and the engine's progress bar, filling with its real progress, move. The background never moves, nothing loops or flashes, and there is no sound.
  - **Controls** are the platform's own: radios, checkboxes, range sliders, selects and buttons, coloured with `accent-color` and never rebuilt. The tabs are buttons with the ARIA tab roles ([ADR 0111](0111-the-page-asks-its-questions-in-tabs-as-claude-code-does.md)).
  - **Materials:** the top bar is a translucent layer with a blur over the scene and the windows, solid under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`. Windows are solid, with shadows deeper for larger ones, and take a solid border under `prefers-contrast: more`.
  - **Type:** tracking and leading are set per size, tighter on large headings and looser on body text, and sizes are in `rem`, so the layout follows the reader's text size.
  - **`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`** turns every animation off: changes appear at once, and the progress bar shows each new value without moving to it. This is stricter than the skill, which lets reduced motion keep short cross-fades: nothing moving is the simplest to test and the strictest reading of the setting.
- Every text pair is at least WCAG AA's 4.5:1, and focus rings and the tabs' glyphs at least 3:1, as the site plan measures them; a translucent layer is measured over the lightest and the darkest surface that can pass under it. Colour is never the only cue. The page's own files stay within 300 KB compressed, and the site plan's CSP holds: no third-party request, and no inline script or style, in HTML or in SVG.

**Why:** Vaporwave's look, old computers under a synthetic dusk, suits an app named like a retro gadget that makes programmed drums sound played. Original artwork and fonts without a reserved name keep a public site clear of others' property, and Apple's principles keep a decorative look calm and quick to use.

**Consequences:** `feat/web-theme` builds the look, and its tests measure each text pair, the page's weight, and that no animation runs under reduced motion. The logo stays the owner's to supply, with no other work's logotype to clear.
