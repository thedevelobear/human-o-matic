# 0125. The page takes the owner's design in Tailwind from its Play CDN, with Google Fonts, the mascot as its logo, and a loading view

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-15

- **The look** is the owner's design, copied with its components: its palette (parchment, cream, ink, cosmic, sunset, magenta, lavender), the paper-grain page, hard ink borders with offset shadows and buttons that press, the iridescent mesh, pill and soft gradients, halftone dots, the perspective grid floor at the page's foot, the stepper of pills, each step a card, each question a fieldset with its legend, each answer a selectable card around its native radio, the retro slider on the native range input, a toast for a round applied, and a `<dialog>` that confirms "Delete my file now". Text of the design that isn't true of the page is left out: a version, an "active session" line, a fixed progress and fixed counts. The song's bars and sections show as opened.
- **Tailwind and Google Fonts:** the page loads Tailwind 3.4.17's Play CDN, pinned in its URL, `https://cdn.tailwindcss.com/3.4.17`, and Google Fonts' stylesheet for Fredoka, Space Grotesk and Space Mono. Tailwind's configuration is a file of the page's own, `js/tailwind-config.js`, loaded right after the CDN's script, and the design's style block is in `site.css`. The page's code stays ES modules of its own over native controls.
- **No integrity hash:** the pinned URL gave the same SHA-384 on three fetches, but its responses carry no `Access-Control-Allow-Origin`, which a browser needs to check an integrity hash on another origin's script, so an `integrity` attribute would stop the script from running. None is set.
- **The CSP** adds only what the two need: `script-src` adds `https://cdn.tailwindcss.com`; `style-src` adds `'unsafe-inline'`, for the `<style>` element the Play CDN writes into the page, and `https://fonts.googleapis.com`; `font-src` adds `https://fonts.gstatic.com`. It gains no `'unsafe-eval'`: the CDN's 3.4.17 file holds no `eval` and no `Function` constructor. A test holds the page's own files to naming no other origin.
- **The mascot,** the owner's CRT with its floppy disk and rubber-hose arm, is the site's logo, drawn once as an inline SVG symbol and shown beside the wordmark "human-o-matic" in Fredoka. Every drawing is hidden from assistive technology.
- **The loading view:** when the page opens, and when "Delete my file now" starts the engine again, the page shows the mascot, the wordmark, the engine's state and the iridescent bar alone. The rest of the page is hidden and inert until the engine is ready, then replaces the view at step 1. The bar is the native `<progress>`: indeterminate until the engine's first step reports, then at the share of the engine's bytes loaded after each of its four steps, as the worker already reports. A failure says why, and offers a reload.
- **Motion:** the mascot bobs, the stars sparkle and the bar shimmers, in loops, as the owner's design has them, where [ADR 0110](0110-the-site-looks-vaporwave-from-original-artwork-shaped-by-apple-s-design-principles.md) let nothing loop; buttons press and the toast slides. Every animation and transition runs only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, and none moves a control.
- **Kept from ADR 0110:** native controls; every text pair at least 4.5:1 and every focus ring at least 3:1, measured by a test from the palette's tokens; colour never the only cue; `prefers-contrast: more`; and the page's own files within 300 KB compressed, with Tailwind's script and Google's fonts outside that count. The pill's last stop is `#9878D0`, where the design's `#8C6BC8` gave ink 4.47:1; the delete button's text is ink on magenta, where white gave 3.55:1; and grey text is ink at 75% at the least, where the design's 50% and 60% on parchment gave 3.30:1 and 4.46:1.

**Why:** The owner asked for the design they supplied, with its components, Tailwind and Google Fonts, and for the engine's loading to show the bar until the forms can be used. A page shown only once the engine can answer has nothing on it that waits for the engine.

**Consequences:** [ADR 0110](0110-the-site-looks-vaporwave-from-original-artwork-shaped-by-apple-s-design-principles.md) is superseded by this ADR; [ADR 0118](0118-the-page-is-hand-rolled-es-modules-with-native-controls-and-no-library.md)'s line that the page uses no library, vendored or fetched, and [ADR 0119](0119-the-build-writes-netlify-s-headers-with-hsts-for-a-year-and-fails-on-a-file-of-10-mb-or-more.md)'s CSP and its page naming no other origin are amended by it. Tailwind's own page says the Play CDN is "designed for development purposes only, and is not the best choice for production". The page needs both hosts online for its look; its own code depends on neither, so without them it should show the same steps with only `site.css`'s styles, in the browser's fonts, which the owner's browser check confirms. The CSP allows inline styles, so a style injected into the page would apply. Each visit sends the visitor's IP address to Tailwind's CDN and to Google, which the page's notice says; the MIDI still never leaves the browser. Tailwind's script is 407 KB, 123 KB compressed, on every first visit. The page's own files measured 39 KB compressed. Building Tailwind's CSS into a file of the site's own would take back the CSP and the requests, at the cost of a build step with Node packages.
