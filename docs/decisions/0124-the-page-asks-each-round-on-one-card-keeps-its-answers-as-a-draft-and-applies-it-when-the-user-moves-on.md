# 0124. The page asks each round on one card, keeps its answers as a draft, and applies it when the user moves on

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-15

- **One card a round.** The Drummer step's song-wide round and each repeat group's round in Sections show all their questions at once. Each is a `<fieldset>` whose `<legend>` is the question, with what its section plays and its pattern, then native radios, each labelled with its number and answer and described by its one-line description: the vocabulary's rows as [ADR 0111](0111-the-page-asks-its-questions-in-tabs-as-claude-code-does.md) maps them, "your call", and "Set it by hand". "Your call" is chosen until another answer is. "Set it by hand" shows that question's own controls under it while it is chosen, starting at the section's resolved values ([ADR 0120](0120-the-question-window-sends-only-the-controls-changed-and-enter-on-set-it-by-hand-opens-its-controls.md)). The round's Fine-tune, every control with the `skip` and `creative` checkboxes, is a disclosure at the card's end, open when a control in it was moved. There are no tabs, no Submit, and no key of the page's own: the radio groups keep the platform's arrow keys, and Tab reaches a question's own controls after its answers.
- **A draft.** A round's answers, the controls moved, in a question's own controls and in Fine-tune, and its Ramp questions are its draft, kept in the page's memory for the song as opened. A round drawn again, from a Next button, the top bar, the section strip or the review, shows its draft. The drafts are dropped when the song is opened again and by "Delete my file now". A section edit keeps the song's draft, and a group's when the edit leaves its sections, and the sections its Ramp questions set, with the same first bar, bars and group and none joining it. It forgets the rest, so a round over changed sections starts afresh at "your call", and its next apply takes back what it set before on the sections left as they were ([ADR 0122](0122-a-round-sent-again-takes-back-what-its-answers-set-before-and-set-no-longer.md)).
- **Applied when the user moves on.** Leaving a round, by its Next button ("Next: sections", "Next group", "Next: review"), a step of the top bar or a section of the strip, applies it when it was never applied, even untouched, or has changed since it was: an answer, a moved control or a question that joined. Applying makes the calls Submit made: `set_style` then `set_section_controls` for the song, or a group's `set_section_controls` for each set of controls, with only the controls moved ([ADR 0120](0120-the-question-window-sends-only-the-controls-changed-and-enter-on-set-it-by-hand-opens-its-controls.md)) and `null` for what its answers set before and set no longer ([ADR 0122](0122-a-round-sent-again-takes-back-what-its-answers-set-before-and-set-no-longer.md)). A round applied and unchanged since sends nothing. A section edit, a take or "Delete my file now" doesn't leave the round.
- **Its state** is a line in a polite live region at the card's end: "Not applied yet: it applies when you move on", with the count of questions so far, or "Applied", with the echo. A round that fails to apply stays, keeps its draft, and says why there and in the page's alert.
- **A Ramp question,** due once both of its groups are applied ([ADR 0108](0108-the-skill-s-budget-is-about-20-questions-with-a-third-per-group-only-for-a-ramp.md)), joins the round whose apply brings it up. When that round's Next button applied it, the round stays, with the question added, focus on its answer and "One more question came up" in its state line, and the next click of the button applies the round again, the ramp with it, and moves on. Left by the top bar or the strip, the page moves on with the ramp unset, and the question joins the next group's round drawn, of whichever group, and is applied with it. A round keeps its Ramp questions in its draft, so a ramp answered can be changed there later.
- **The count** is [ADR 0090](0090-the-skill-counts-its-questions-the-confirmation-batch-as-one.md)'s: one for the kits, one for the check batch, and one for each question the first time its round is applied.
- **The review** lists each round's answers in words, "Set it by hand" with the controls it moved, and the controls Fine-tune moved, each round with a button back to it to change them, and each group not asked yet with a button to answer it.

**Why:** Tabs after Claude Code's prompt made a web user submit each round and lost what they chose when they didn't. The guidance read, GOV.UK Design System's question pages, check answers and validation patterns, W3C WAI's multi-page forms tutorial, Nielsen Norman Group's articles on wizards and on errors in forms, and Material Design's steppers, keeps what was entered when the user goes back, moves on with one button, keeps the user on the page with what they entered when something fails, asks a question a change brings up before going on, and ends on one summary where each answer can be changed; Apple's page on steppers didn't render, and its stepper sets a number. Native radio groups need no key of the page's own, and an unchanged round sends nothing.

**Consequences:** [ADR 0111](0111-the-page-asks-its-questions-in-tabs-as-claude-code-does.md)'s window of tabs, Submit, keyboard and ARIA, and its line that only Submit sends; [ADR 0120](0120-the-question-window-sends-only-the-controls-changed-and-enter-on-set-it-by-hand-opens-its-controls.md)'s Enter on "Set it by hand", its Submit tab and its round drawn again starting afresh; [ADR 0123](0123-the-page-reads-each-question-s-tab-from-its-wording-in-get-sections.md)'s Ramp tab joining a round only once it is sent; and [ADR 0110](0110-the-site-looks-vaporwave-from-original-artwork-shaped-by-apple-s-design-principles.md)'s tabs are amended by this ADR. `tabs.js` and its tests go. Leaving a round applies it even when only another group's Ramp question joined it, and a section of the same group picked on the strip applies the round too. An edit that changes a group's sections forgets its whole draft, so answers to questions it still asks start again at "your call". A group's rows over the song's tightness or feel are taken when the group is applied, so a later change to the song-wide round doesn't apply the groups again. A ramp left unset by the top bar stays unset if no group's round is applied after it, as before. The look of the cards follows on the same branch.
