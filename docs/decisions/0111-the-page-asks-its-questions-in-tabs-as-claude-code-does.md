# 0111. The page asks its questions in tabs, as Claude Code does

- **Status:** Accepted. Its window of tabs, its Submit, its keyboard and ARIA, and its line that only Submit sends are amended by [ADR 0124](0124-the-page-asks-each-round-on-one-card-keeps-its-answers-as-a-draft-and-applies-it-when-the-user-moves-on.md)
- **Date:** 2026-11-14

- A round of questions is one window: a strip of tabs, one for each question and a last Submit tab, then the current question with what its section plays and its pattern, then its numbered answers, each with a one-line description and a pointer on the current one, and last, "Set it by hand". A question's tab shows a box until it is answered, then a check. The song-wide round, in the Drummer step, and each repeat group's round, in the Sections step, use it; the page's other steps are windows of their own, without tabs. The site plan's section 6 draws it, maps each tab's answers and gives its keyboard and ARIA in full.
- **Tabs and answers.** The song-wide round has two tabs: Drummer, whose style rows set the style and its tightness, and Feel. A group's tabs are every question `get_sections` gives its sections ([ADR 0113](0113-there-is-no-question-budget-questions-are-asked-as-the-engine-suggests-them-counted-and-never-limited.md)), and a group whose sections ask nothing has no round. A tab's answers are the vocabulary's rows that answer its question, named by their answer text, then "your call".
  - The Energy tab has the 11 section kinds only. An energy alone, as "full power" or "fairly hard, not all out" gives it, is set through "Set it by hand".
  - The Ramp tab offers a choice of bars up to what the section allows: the vocabulary's "build into it over 2 bars" and "a 4-bar ramp" where the section allows that many, any other number through "Set it by hand", and "no ramp, straight in".
- **Rows over the song's controls,** as the vocabulary's "How to read a row" sets: where the song has its own value, such as the Drummer tab's tightness or the Feel tab's feel, a section's control is the song's value moved by the row's difference from the style's default, and stays unset where the row gives the default.
- **"Your call"** sets nothing new, as the skill's rule has it. On the Energy tab it takes the row for what the section is when the user renamed the section on the page and the name, trimmed and compared ignoring case, equals a section kind's answer text up to its first comma, for exactly one kind: equality, not a prefix. Otherwise the section keeps the song's controls, or a repeat its group's. On the other tabs it sets none of that tab's controls itself, and on the Ramp tab it is `ramp: 0`, as [ADR 0108](0108-the-skill-s-budget-is-about-20-questions-with-a-third-per-group-only-for-a-ramp.md) sets.
- **"Set it by hand"** takes the place of Claude Code's "Type something": with no LLM ([ADR 0097](0097-a-website-is-milestone-m7-a-thin-page-over-the-same-engine-that-asks-the-questions-itself.md)), a typed answer can't be read. It opens in the tab the Fine-tune controls of that question alone, starting at the section's resolved values, and is the one answer the page names that isn't a row.
- **Only Submit sends.** It sends the whole round as it stands, each control replacing the one it set before, with a question left unanswered as "your call". Next and the tabs send nothing. A ramp question `get_sections` gives once a round is sent joins that round as a Ramp tab, sent at its next Submit; if the user moves on first, the ramp stays unset and the question joins the next round sent.
- **The count** is [ADR 0090](0090-the-skill-counts-its-questions-the-confirmation-batch-as-one.md)'s: one for the kits window, one for the check batch, and one for each question tab the first time it is sent, whatever its answer. It is shown on the Submit tab and in the review, and limits nothing.
- **Keyboard and ARIA:** a click or Enter answers; the arrow and number keys only move the choice. Left and Right move between tabs on the strip, and stay the radio group's own in the answers, as the platform defines them, rather than move between tabs as in Claude Code. Shift+Tab back to the strip, or its left arrow button, goes back. The tabs follow the WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices' tabs pattern, with a tab showing its panel as soon as it has focus.

**Why:** Claude Code's prompt puts a few questions with set answers one at a time, with the round's state in view, and the page's questions are just that. Without an LLM, the free answer becomes the controls themselves. Native radios keep the keys their users expect, and one button that sends keeps the echo true to what the engine holds.

**Consequences:** `feat/web-ui` builds the window and tests its keyboard and ARIA through pure modules under `node --test` ([ADR 0112](0112-node-with-no-npm-package-runs-the-pyodide-test-and-the-page-s-pure-module-tests.md)). `feat/web-questions` builds the rounds from section 6's mapping and tests that every answer the page names but "Set it by hand" is a row. The Energy tab's 13 answers are more than Claude Code's prompts show, and the number keys reach its first nine. A group with many questions has many tabs, and the strip wraps.
