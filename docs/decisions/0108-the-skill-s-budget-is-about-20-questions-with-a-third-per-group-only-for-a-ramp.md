# 0108. The skill's budget is about 20 questions, with a third per group only to ask about a ramp into a section 0.2 or more louder

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-14

- The question policy's budget is about 20 questions for a typical song, where it was about 12. They are counted as [ADR 0090](0090-the-skill-counts-its-questions-the-confirmation-batch-as-one.md) sets: the kits question when asked, the confirmation batch as one, each other question as one, and the echo's "sound right?" not at all.
- Each repeat group gets at most two questions, and a third only to ask whether its sections build into a louder section after them, as a `ramp` ([ADR 0107](0107-a-section-s-ramp-climbs-its-last-bars-toward-the-energy-the-next-section-starts-at.md)). No other question can be a third.
- `get_sections` adds the ramp question to a section's `questions`, after the facts' own, when the section and the next both have notes, neither is skipped, the section has 2 bars or more, its `ramp` is unset in its own and the song's controls, and the next section's `energy` is 0.2 or more above its own, each its own over the song's over the style's default, the difference rounded to 6 places (`RAMP_STEP` in `mcp_server.py`). It reads "Build up into bars 5 to 8 over the last bars of bars 1 to 4, each a little harder, or go straight in? How many bars, up to 2?", up to `min(8, bars // 2)`.
- The skill calls `get_sections` after each group's answer, and asks a ramp question once both groups, the ramping section's and the louder one's, have been asked about, right after the later answer, in the user's terms. It asks once per group, setting the answer on every section of the group that leads into the louder group, and once for a group that leads into more than one, naming them.
- "No", "none", "straight in" and "your call" are `ramp: 0`, always accepted, and set so the question isn't suggested again.
- `references/vocabulary.md` gains a `ramp` row in "In words" and three answers in the `Also` column: "build into it over 2 bars", `ramp: 2 [2, 2]`, "a 4-bar ramp", `ramp: 4 [4, 4]`, and "no ramp, straight in", `ramp: 0 [0, 0]`. `tools/check_answers.py` reads a ramp's numbers as whole bars and words each answer in a phrase of its own, so the table has 37 answers and 3 refinements.

**Why:** Ramps need the louder section's energy, which only its group's answer gives, so the question comes after it and costs at most one per group. Four groups at three each, three song-wide questions, the batch and the kits make 17, and a repeat or two that differ make about 20. The vocabulary's ranges reach about 0.1 either side of an answer's energy, so sections 0.2 or more apart are clearly two answers, not one read a little higher. At 0.2 a verse at 0.55 into a chorus at 0.8, a bridge at 0.35 into it and a quiet intro at 0.3 into a verse at 0.5 are asked about, while a pre-chorus at 0.65, which builds by its arc, into the chorus, and the chorus into a big last one at 0.9 aren't.

**Consequences:** [ADR 0090](0090-the-skill-counts-its-questions-the-confirmation-batch-as-one.md)'s budget of about 12 is about 20, the plan's question policy changes in rules 3 and 7, and M7's page takes the same budget and ramp question from the skill. [ADR 0082](0082-a-section-s-facts-are-counted-and-its-guess-says-only-what-the-score-shows.md)'s questions gain one read from the controls, not the score. The `Also` column of [ADR 0087](0087-the-skill-s-answers-are-checked-by-a-table-with-ranges-a-test-of-it-and-an-agent-given-only-the-skill.md) takes `ramp`, and the count of answers of [ADR 0089](0089-the-vocabulary-s-rows-set-only-their-controls-and-beyond-the-plan-s-four-are-drafted-unheard.md) and [ADR 0096](0096-the-skill-turns-creative-mode-on-only-when-the-user-asks-for-added-notes.md) is 37. The agent pass has three more phrases to read as the vocabulary means them, and whether 20 questions feel worth it is the owner's to say after a real song.
