# 0111. The page asks its questions in tabs, as Claude Code does

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-14

- A round of questions is one window. At its top, a strip of tabs, one for each question of the round and a last tab, Submit, between a left and a right arrow button. A question's tab shows a box until it is answered, then a check. Below the strip, the current question beside a bar, with what its section plays and its pattern, then its answers, numbered, each with a one-line description and a pointer on the current one, and last, "Set it by hand".
- The song-wide round, the Drummer step, and each repeat group's round, in song order in the Sections step, use it. The page's other steps, the file, the kits, the check batch, the review, the download and the feedback, are windows of their own in the same look, without tabs. Section edits (split, merge, move and rename) and another take stay in a Sections window above the round, as the site plan has them, and its section strip opens any group's round again.
- The answers are the vocabulary's rows, named by their answer text. A group's tabs are the questions `get_sections` gives its sections, in this mapping, and a group whose sections ask nothing, such as a silent one, has no round:

  | Round | Tab | When | Answers | Set on |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Song-wide | Drummer | Always | "tight, modern metal", "somewhere in between", "loose, like a live drummer", "machine tight, nearly quantized", "your call" | The song, by `set_style` |
  | Song-wide | Feel | Always | "laid back", "way behind the beat, lazy", "pushing, on top of the beat", "your call" | The song, by `set_section_controls` without `section_ids` |
  | A group | Energy | Its first section is a groove | Every row that sets `energy`, the 11 section kinds, "full power" and "fairly hard, not all out", from the loudest to the quietest by their starting energy, ties in the vocabulary's order, then "your call" | The group's sections |
  | A group | Soft notes | That groove has soft notes | "soft notes more present", "soft notes tucked away", "your call" | The group's sections |
  | A group | Groove | That groove has riders and no soft notes | "steady and even", "more groove, more light and shade", "your call" | The group's sections |
  | A group | Fill | Its first section is a fill | "fill, big into the chorus", "small fill, just a turnaround", "your call" | The group's sections |
  | A group | Last repeat | `get_sections` asks "the same, or bigger?" of its last repeat | "your call", the same as the group, "a bit harder", added to the group's energy, and "big last chorus" | That section alone |
  | A group | Ramp | `get_sections` gives the ramp question to its sections, and both it and the louder group after it have been asked about ([ADR 0108](0108-the-skill-s-budget-is-about-20-questions-with-a-third-per-group-only-for-a-ramp.md)) | "build into it over 2 bars" and "a 4-bar ramp", each only where the section allows that many bars, "no ramp, straight in", "your call" | Every section of that group that leads into the louder one |

- A row's description is the controls it sets, in plain words with their starting points from the `presets` call, such as "energy 0.8, groove 0.5, soft notes 0.35". The pointer starts on the answer given before, or else on the first.
- "Your call" sets nothing, but on the Ramp tab, where it is `ramp: 0`, as [ADR 0108](0108-the-skill-s-budget-is-about-20-questions-with-a-third-per-group-only-for-a-ramp.md) sets.
- "Set it by hand" takes the place of Claude Code's "Type something": with no LLM ([ADR 0097](0097-a-website-is-milestone-m7-a-thin-page-over-the-same-engine-that-asks-the-questions-itself.md)), a typed answer can't be read. It opens in the tab the Fine-tune sliders of that question's controls, starting at the section's resolved values: style and `tightness` for Drummer, `feel` for Feel, `energy` and `arc` for Energy, `ghost_level` for Soft notes, `groove` and `dynamic_range` for Groove, `fill_intensity` for Fill, `energy` for Last repeat, and `ramp` from 0 to the section's most for Ramp. It is the one answer the page names that isn't a row.
- The Submit tab lists each answer and the controls it sets, with a question left unanswered as "your call". It holds Fine-tune, every control of the round as sliders and checkboxes starting at what the answers set, and, in a group's round, the checkboxes "Leave this section as it is", `skip`, and "Add notes", `creative`, off by default and never raised by a question ([ADR 0096](0096-the-skill-turns-creative-mode-on-only-when-the-user-asks-for-added-notes.md)). It shows the questions asked so far against the budget of about 20. Its button sends the round, then shows the echo in a polite live region: the reply's `expected` in words, for each section the round set, or each group's first section after the song-wide round, and each control in `clamped` with the value used. After a group's round is sent, the page calls `get_sections`, and each ramp question it now gives joins the round as a Ramp tab before Submit. Next opens the next group's round.
- The count is [ADR 0090](0090-the-skill-counts-its-questions-the-confirmation-batch-as-one.md)'s: one for the kits window, one for the check batch, and one for each question tab when its round is first sent, whatever its answer. Submit, the echo, Fine-tune, section edits and takes count none.
- **Keyboard:** on the strip, Left and Right move between tabs, wrapping at the ends, Home and End go to the first and last, and a tab shows its panel as soon as it has focus. In the answers, a native radio group, the arrow keys move the choice as the platform defines, the number keys 1 to 9 choose that answer, and Enter answers and opens the next tab with focus on its current answer. The arrow buttons go to the previous and next tab. Left and Right stay the radios' own in the answers, rather than move between tabs as in Claude Code.
- **ARIA,** as the WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices' tabs pattern defines it: the strip is a `tablist`, labelled with the round's name, of `<button role="tab">` elements. The current tab has `aria-selected="true"` and `tabindex="0"`, the others `aria-selected="false"` and `tabindex="-1"`, and each has `aria-controls` naming its `tabpanel`, which has `aria-labelledby` naming the tab. The answers are a `<fieldset>` whose `<legend>` is the question, each radio's `<label>` holding its number and answer, and its description tied to it by `aria-describedby`. The box, the check, the pointer and the arrows are SVG hidden from assistive technology, so an answered tab says "answered" in its name. The arrow buttons have labels of their own and sit outside the `tablist`.

**Why:** Claude Code's prompt puts a few questions with set answers one at a time, with the round's state in view, and the page's questions are just that. Without an LLM, the free answer becomes the controls themselves.

**Consequences:** `feat/web-ui` builds the tabbed window and tests its keyboard and ARIA through pure modules under `node --test` ([ADR 0112](0112-node-with-no-npm-package-runs-the-pyodide-test-and-the-page-s-pure-module-tests.md)). `feat/web-questions` builds the rounds and tests that every answer the page names but "Set it by hand" is a row. The page asks every question `get_sections` gives, where the skill may leave one out as unlikely to change the controls audibly. The Energy tab, at 15 answers, is longer than Claude Code's prompts, and the number keys reach only its first nine.
