Your file and your feedback

Your MIDI file stays on your computer: it is processed in your browser and never uploaded. If you send feedback, we keep what you write and the kits and settings you used, until we delete it; the page never sends your file, its name or its notes. Your feedback is stored by our host, Netlify, which also records your IP address with it and checks it for spam with Akismet. Our host may keep request logs, under its own policy. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when it sends them. Only use files you have the right to use.
