# 0107. A section's `ramp` climbs its last bars toward the energy the next section starts at

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-14

- `ramp` is a control of `ControlVector`, `ControlInput` and `Controls` (`controls.py`): a whole number of bars from 0 to 8, `MAX_RAMP`, 0 in every style's defaults and in the placeholder, and unset in older sessions and plans. Set per section with `set_section_controls`, or for the song without `section_ids`, under each section's own. An agent's number is rounded to a whole bar and clamped to 0 to 8, and listed in `clamped`.
- A section plays its ramp over its last `min(ramp, bars // 2)` bars (`ramp_bars`). `set_section_controls` gives that value in `resolved`, and when the patch sets `ramp` and the half binds, adds to `clamped` an entry with the section's first bar as `section`, beside the value stored and the value used. The stored value stays as given, so an edit that lengthens the section lengthens its ramp.
- **Target:** the energy the next section starts at: its `energy`, its own over the song's over the style's default, moved by its arc at its start, the style's arc span less when it builds and more when it drops.
- **Curve:** from `begin`, the energy the section's own arc gives where the ramp starts, ramp bar `k` of `n`, from 1, plays at `begin + (target - begin) * (k / (n + 1)) ** 2` for the whole bar, each note by its grid point. Each step is bigger than the one before it, the last ramp bar is a step short of the target, and the next section's downbeat is the last and biggest step. The shares of the way are 0.111 and 0.444 for 2 bars; 0.040, 0.160, 0.360 and 0.640 for 4; and 0.012, 0.049, 0.111, 0.198, 0.309, 0.444, 0.605 and 0.790 for 8. `RAMP_POWER`, 2, is one constant in `render/compiler.py`.
- **Arc:** the arc shapes the section up to the ramp and sets where it starts, and in the ramp's bars the ramp's energy is used in place of the arc's. Every energy stays between `begin` and the target, so within what the arcs of the two sections already reach.
- **Only up:** no ramp plays when the target isn't above `begin`, when the next section is skipped or plays no note, or out of the song's last section, and a skipped section plays none. A section that falls away is still `arc: drop`.
- **Fills and the rest:** everything the energy sets follows the ramp bar's energy: velocity centers and floors, accents and the soft notes' center. A fill in a ramp bar takes its energy, under its `fill_intensity` shift and crescendo (Appendix A, "Fills"), and so does a fill a creative section adds ([ADR 0092](0092-a-creative-section-adds-by-rules-read-from-its-own-score.md)), drawn through its section's parameters ([ADR 0093](0093-added-notes-draw-from-a-stream-of-their-own-through-their-section-s-parameters.md)).
- **No draw:** a ramp draws nothing, so every stream and draw is as [ADR 0049](0049-each-section-draws-from-its-own-seeded-stream.md) sets them, which this keeps whole. With `ramp` 0 or unset a render is byte for byte as it was without the control.
- **Independence:** a section with a ramp reads the next section's `energy`, `arc` and `skip`, never its draws or its take. So another take of a section leaves every other section's notes as they were, ramps included, and new controls for a section leave every other section's notes as they were but the section before it, when that one has a ramp, whose ramp bars follow. This is a second exception beside `skip`'s to [ADR 0050](0050-guards-hold-every-backend-and-keep-each-section-independent.md)'s section independence, to [ADR 0052](0052-each-control-is-tested-against-the-statistic-it-mainly-drives.md)'s property of one section rendered again, and to the one exception [ADR 0094](0094-an-added-note-changes-no-other-section.md) names. The guards' rules at a boundary still come from the score alone.
- **Words:** `render/describe.py` adds, for a section whose ramp plays, "builds over bars 13 to 16, its hits about 101, 104, 107 and 110 bar by bar", the mean velocity of its normal-typed notes in each ramp bar (`measure.bar_velocities`), to `set_section_controls`' `expected` and the `says` of `render` and `export`.
- **Tests,** with `hypothesis` where drawn: `ramp` 0, on drawn sections and the song, gives the bytes of the same plan without it; the energies rise by a bigger step each bar, from above `begin` to below the target, the step into the target the biggest, and are the arc's before the ramp; a ramp plays over `min(ramp, bars // 2)` bars exactly when the next section plays, isn't skipped and starts higher; another take of a section leaves every other as it was, and new controls every other but a ramping section before it. Examples, averaged over 8 seeds: each ramp bar's normal notes are louder than the bar before, the first step the smallest, and the last ramp bar below the next section's first; a fill in the last ramp bar, and a fill creative mode adds there, are louder than with no ramp; the clamps; and no ramp into a skipped, silent or quieter section or out of the last.

**Why:** A drummer pushing into a louder section builds over its last bars, and the fills there with it. A curve that starts slowly and speeds up, a step short of the next section, leaves that section's downbeat the peak. Reading the next section's controls, not its draws, keeps every stream and every take independent, and drawing nothing keeps `ramp` 0 exactly as before.

**Consequences:** [ADR 0046](0046-controls-are-layered-pydantic-models-with-a-json-round-trip.md)'s controls, ten since [ADR 0091](0091-creative-mode-is-a-section-s-control-off-by-default-that-lets-it-add-notes.md), are eleven. Changing a section's energy, arc or skip can change the bars of the section before it. The curve and its power are drafted unheard: how a ramp sounds is the owner's to judge by ear.
