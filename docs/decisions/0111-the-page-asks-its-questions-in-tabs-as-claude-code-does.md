# 0111. The page asks its questions in tabs, as Claude Code does

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-14

- A round of questions is one window. At its top, a strip of tabs, one for each question of the round and a last tab, Submit, between a left and a right arrow button. A question's tab shows a box until it is answered, then a check. Below the strip, the current question beside a bar, with what its section plays and its pattern, then its answers, numbered, each with a one-line description and a pointer on the current one, and last, "Set it by hand".
- The song-wide round, the Drummer step, and each repeat group's round, in song order in the Sections step, use it. The page's other steps, the file, the kits, the check batch, the review, the download and the feedback, are windows of their own in the same look, without tabs. Section edits (split, merge, move and rename) and another take stay in a Sections window above the round, and its section strip opens any group's round again.
- **Tabs and answers.** The song-wide round has two tabs: Drummer, whose style rows set the style and its tightness, and Feel. A group's tabs are the questions `get_sections` gives its sections, and a group whose sections ask nothing, such as a silent one, has no round. Each tab's answers are the vocabulary's rows that answer its question, named by their answer text, then "your call", as the site plan's section 6 maps them:
  - The Energy tab has the 11 section kinds only, from the loudest to the quietest by their starting energy, ties in the vocabulary's order. An energy alone, as "full power" or "fairly hard, not all out" gives it, is set through "Set it by hand".
  - The Ramp tab offers a choice of bars up to what the section allows, as the vocabulary's "build into it over 2 bars" and "a 4-bar ramp" where the section allows that many and any other number through "Set it by hand", and "no ramp, straight in".
  - A row's description is the controls it sets, in plain words with their starting points from the `presets` call, such as "energy 0.8, groove 0.5, soft notes 0.35". The pointer starts on the answer given before, or else on the first.
- **Rows over the song's controls,** as the vocabulary's "How to read a row" sets: rows are written for a song whose own controls are unset. Where the song has its own value, such as the Drummer tab's tightness or the Feel tab's feel, the page sets a section's control at the song's value moved by the row's difference from the style's default, as `set_style`'s reply gives it, and leaves the control unset where the row gives the default.
- **"Your call"** follows the skill's rule: it sets nothing new. On the Energy tab it takes the row for what the section is when the user named the section by a rename on the page and the name, ignoring case, is a section kind's answer text up to its first comma for exactly one kind; otherwise the section keeps the song's controls, or a repeat its group's. On the other tabs it sets none of that tab's controls itself. The echo says which. On the Ramp tab it is `ramp: 0`, as [ADR 0108](0108-the-skill-s-budget-is-about-20-questions-with-a-third-per-group-only-for-a-ramp.md) and the vocabulary set.
- **"Set it by hand"** takes the place of Claude Code's "Type something": with no LLM ([ADR 0097](0097-a-website-is-milestone-m7-a-thin-page-over-the-same-engine-that-asks-the-questions-itself.md)), a typed answer can't be read. It opens in the tab the Fine-tune controls of that question, starting at the section's resolved values: style and `tightness` for Drummer, `feel` for Feel, `energy` and `arc` for Energy, `ghost_level` for Soft notes, `groove` and `dynamic_range` for Groove, `fill_intensity` for Fill, `energy` for Last repeat, and `ramp` from 0 to the section's most for Ramp. It is the one answer the page names that isn't a row.
- **Submit** lists each answer and the controls it sets, a question left unanswered as "your call". It holds Fine-tune, every control of the round as sliders and checkboxes starting at what the answers set, and, in a group's round, the checkboxes "Leave this section as it is", `skip`, and "Add notes", `creative`, off by default and never raised by a question ([ADR 0096](0096-the-skill-turns-creative-mode-on-only-when-the-user-asks-for-added-notes.md)). It shows the questions asked so far against the budget of about 20. Its button sends the whole round as it stands, every time, each control replacing the one set before, then shows the echo in a polite live region: the reply's `expected` in words, for each section the round set, or each group's first section after the song-wide round, and each control in `clamped` with the value used. Next opens the next group's round.
- **The Ramp tab.** Once a group's round is sent, the page calls `get_sections`. Each ramp question it now gives, once both the group that would ramp and the louder group after it have been asked about, joins the round just sent, the later of the two to be asked about, as a Ramp tab before Submit. Its answer is set on every section of the group that leads into the louder one, which may be an earlier group's, and is sent with the round the next time Submit sends it. The polite live region says the tab has joined, and focus stays where it is.
- **The count** is [ADR 0090](0090-the-skill-counts-its-questions-the-confirmation-batch-as-one.md)'s: one for the kits window, one for the check batch, and one for each question tab the first time it is sent, whatever its answer. Sending a round again, Submit, the echo, Fine-tune, section edits and takes count none.
- **Answering:** a tab is answered when an answer is chosen by a click or by Enter. The arrow and number keys only move the choice. A click stays on the tab; Enter opens the next tab with focus on its current answer, and on the last question tab opens Submit with focus on its first control.
- **Keyboard:** on the strip, Left and Right move between tabs, wrapping at the ends, Home and End go to the first and last, and a tab shows its panel as soon as it has focus. In the answers, a native radio group, the arrow keys move the choice as the platform defines, Left and Right included, rather than move between tabs as in Claude Code, and the number keys 1 to 9 move it to that answer. The number keys are ignored while focus is in a slider or another field of "Set it by hand". To go back, Shift+Tab returns to the strip, where Left moves to the previous tab, or the strip's left arrow button does it.
- **ARIA,** as the WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices' tabs pattern defines it: the strip is a `tablist`, labelled with the round's name, of `<button role="tab">` elements. The current tab has `aria-selected="true"` and `tabindex="0"`, the others `aria-selected="false"` and `tabindex="-1"`, and each has `aria-controls` naming its `tabpanel`, which has `aria-labelledby` naming the tab. The answers are a `<fieldset>` whose `<legend>` is the question, each radio's `<label>` holding its number and answer, and its description tied to it by `aria-describedby`. The box, the check, the pointer and the arrows are SVG hidden from assistive technology, so an answered tab says "answered" in its name. The arrow buttons have labels of their own and sit outside the `tablist`.

**Why:** Claude Code's prompt puts a few questions with set answers one at a time, with the round's state in view, and the page's questions are just that. Without an LLM, the free answer becomes the controls themselves.

**Consequences:** `feat/web-ui` builds the tabbed window and tests its keyboard and ARIA through pure modules under `node --test` ([ADR 0112](0112-node-with-no-npm-package-runs-the-pyodide-test-and-the-page-s-pure-module-tests.md)). `feat/web-questions` builds the rounds from section 6's mapping and tests that every answer the page names but "Set it by hand" is a row. The page asks every question `get_sections` gives, where the skill may leave one out as unlikely to change the controls audibly. The Energy tab, at 13 answers, is longer than Claude Code's prompts, and the number keys reach only its first nine.
